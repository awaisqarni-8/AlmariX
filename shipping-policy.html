Shipping & Delivery Policy

Delivery Areas

AlmariX offers delivery all over Pakistan.

Expected Delivery Time

Orders are normally expected to be delivered within 3 to 5 working days.

Delivery Charges

Free delivery is available on AlmariX orders. No additional delivery charges are currently applied.

Payment Method

AlmariX currently accepts Cash on Delivery (COD) only. Customers pay for their order when it is delivered.

Delivery Information

Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address and a valid phone number when placing an order.
