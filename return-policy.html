Return & Refund Policy

AlmariX allows returns within 7 days of delivery in the following cases:

  • Wrong product received
  • Different product received from the one ordered
  • Damaged product received
  • Incomplete delivery/order

For a return request, the customer must contact AlmariX within 7 days of receiving the order.

For damaged, wrong, or incomplete products, the customer should send clear photos of the product showing the problem to our official AlmariX email:

almarixofficial@gmail.com

The photos may be used to verify the return request.

After verification, AlmariX will provide the appropriate return, replacement, or refund solution according to the case.

Important

The product should be kept in its original condition and packaging where applicable.
